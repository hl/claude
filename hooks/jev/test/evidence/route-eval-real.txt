## Reading
- Zero under-routing of opus-labelled tasks and zero sonnet->haiku, in the tuning rows, in a fresh
  3-run eval with the chosen defaults, and in both holdout folds.
- Over-routing is 35%: 316 of 558 sonnet-labelled rows and 66 of 453 haiku-labelled rows go to opus,
  94 haiku rows to sonnet. That is the price of the margin: tune.sh counts under-routing with every
  Jev value moved 0.1 toward the cheaper tier. Without the margin (0.02) the best zero-under point
  over-routes 30%; Jev's raw choice over-routes 24% but sends 3 opus rows lower.
- The mechanical and confidence gates came out at 0 (off): on this data they never separated tiers
  better than P(opus) and the security/review signals. P(haiku) >= 0.5 suffices for haiku: even at
  0.4 (the margin) no sonnet- or opus-labelled row became haiku.
- Weak-label recall is low (27 of 93 rows; 33 of the 93 already ran on opus or fable, so they could
  not go higher). The weak labels are noisy; the blind labels are the measure.
- What actually ran (the model given, or the parent's when none) under-routed 8 of 429 tasks
  (6 opus-labelled) and over-routed 21%.
